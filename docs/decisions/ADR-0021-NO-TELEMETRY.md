# ADR-0021 — No telemetry by default

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Context

The appliance should remain local-first and simple to trust.

## Decision

Ship without analytics/telemetry. Diagnostics are local and explicitly exported by the user.

## Consequences

Lower privacy/network complexity. Product decisions rely on explicit user reports and opt-in future mechanisms.

## Alternatives considered

Silent or mandatory analytics were rejected.
