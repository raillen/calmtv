# Known / Planned Limitations

Until evidence expands support:
- amd64 only;
- 2 GB is target minimum, not a promise for every website/workload;
- 720p/1080p focus;
- 4K not guaranteed;
- commercial web streaming quality/DRM varies by service;
- modern console emulation not baseline;
- unusual IPTV auth/token flows may require provider-specific support;
- CEC depends on compatible hardware/adapter;
- no photo gallery, RSS/news or ambient screensaver mode;
- generic browser is optional, not default.
